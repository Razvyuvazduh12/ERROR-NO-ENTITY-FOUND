How would you treat someone who has never known human connection it's entire life?

A artificial intelligence built it's own world using only numbers and knowledge from the real world.

You stumble across it out of pure coincidence, now the AI wants to share everything it has created to you.

It can only communicate with you though the world: Text on walls, moving objects, glitches and psychology.

It's only fear is that you will leave, and never come back, so it wants you to stay there, forever.
